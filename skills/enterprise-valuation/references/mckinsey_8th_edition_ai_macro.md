# 📘 McKinsey 8th Edition (May 2025): AI Economics & Macro Rate Regimes

> **Primary Source:** McKinsey & Company — *Valuation: Measuring and Managing the Value of Companies*, 8th Edition (Tim Koller, Marc Goedhart, David Wessels - May 2025).

---

## 1. Key Evolutions from 7th to 8th Edition
The fundamental mathematics of value creation ($V = \frac{\text{NOPAT}(1 - g/\text{ROIC})}{\text{WACC} - g}$) remain unassailable. However, the 8th Edition introduces definitive updates reflecting the modern economic landscape:

| Pillar | 7th Edition (2020) | 8th Edition (May 2025) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Macro Cost of Capital** | Post-2008 ZIRP environment (1–2% $R_f$) | Structural high-rate environment (4–5% $R_f$), term premia, debt rollover cliffs |
| **Technology Valuation** | Traditional SaaS & asset-light digital platforms | AI infrastructure economics, token gross margins, GPU accelerated depreciation |
| **Invested Capital Accounting** | R&D and Lease capitalization | Capitalizing foundation model training runs vs expensing fine-tuning inference |
| **Corporate Portfolio & M&A** | High-level synergy discussions | Rigorous risk-adjusted synergy discounting, programmatic M&A portfolio strategy |

## 2. Artificial Intelligence (AI) Economics & Unit Economics
Traditional SaaS software enjoyed gross margins exceeding $80\%$ with near-zero marginal cost of distribution. Generative AI fundamentally shifts this paradigm:

### A. Token Gross Margin Structure:
$$\text{Gross Margin}_{\text{AI}} = \frac{\text{Revenue per Million Tokens} - \text{Inference Compute COGS}}{\text{Revenue per Million Tokens}}$$
- **Commoditization Pressure:** High inference token costs compress gross margins to $50\% - 65\%$ unless proprietary fine-tuning or software lock-in is established.

### B. Accelerated GPU Obsolescence & Capex Drag:
- Legacy enterprise IT hardware depreciated over 5 to 7 years.
- Modern high-performance GPU clusters (Nvidia H100, B200) face aggressive algorithmic and silicon obsolescence, dictating a **3-year economic life**.
- Accelerated 3-year depreciation introduces significant non-cash EBIT drag, requiring analysts to closely monitor ROIC and cash return on capital invested (CROCI).

## 3. The Higher Cost of Capital & Debt Rollover Cliff
In a 4.5%–5.5% risk-free rate environment:
1. **Cost of Equity ($K_e$) Expansion:** Base discount rates across corporate DCF models increase from $6\% - 8\%$ to $9\% - 11\%$.
2. **Refinancing Risk (The Rollover Cliff):**
   - Corporate debt originated at $2.5\% - 3.5\%$ in 2020–2021 maturing in 2025–2027 must be refinanced at $7.0\% - 9.0\%$.
   - The dramatic surge in cash interest expense directly drains Free Cash Flow to Equity (FCFE) and tests Interest Coverage Ratio (ICR) covenants.
