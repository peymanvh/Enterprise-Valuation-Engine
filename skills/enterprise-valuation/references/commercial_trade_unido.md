# 📘 UNIDO Industrial Feasibility & Commercial Trade Standards

> **Primary Source:** United Nations Industrial Development Organization (UNIDO) — *Manual for the Preparation of Industrial Feasibility Studies* (W. Behrens & P.M. Hawranek).

---

## 1. The Dynamic Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)
The Cash Conversion Cycle measures the net operational time required to convert trade outlays back into liquid cash:

$$\text{CCC} = \text{DSO} + \text{DIO} - \text{DPO}$$

Where:
- **Days Sales Outstanding (DSO):**
  $$\text{DSO} = \frac{\text{Accounts Receivable}}{\text{Gross Revenue}} \times 365$$
- **Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO):**
  $$\text{DIO} = \frac{\text{Average Inventory}}{\text{Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)}} \times 365$$
- **Days Payables Outstanding (DPO):**
  $$\text{DPO} = \frac{\text{Accounts Payable}}{\text{COGS or Raw Material Purchases}} \times 365$$

A positive CCC requires working capital financing; a negative CCC indicates customer-funded operations.

## 2. Trade Finance Instruments & Carrying Costs
International commercial trade involves specialized banking facilities:
- **Letters of Credit (LC):** Issuance, confirmation, and acceptance fees ($0.75\% - 2.50\%$ annualized).
- **Bill Discounting / Factoring:** Pre-financing export receivables at short-term benchmark rates plus factoring spread.
- **FX Hedging Reserves:** Forward contract spreads, currency option premiums, and collateral buffers for cross-border currency volatility.

---

## 3. Cash Flow Available for Debt Service (CFADS)
The primary metric in project finance underwriting:

$$\text{CFADS}_t = \text{EBITDA}_t - \text{Cash Taxes}_t - \Delta \text{Working Capital}_t - \text{Maintenance Capex}_t - \text{Trade Finance Fees}_t$$

---

## 4. Bankability & Debt Coverage Ratios

### A. Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR):
$$\text{DSCR}_t = \frac{\text{CFADS}_t}{\text{Principal}_t + \text{Interest}_t}$$
- **Institutional Standard:** Minimum $\text{DSCR} \ge 1.30x$, Average $\text{DSCR} \ge 1.50x$.

### B. Loan Life Coverage Ratio (LLCR):
Measures the project's ability to service the loan over the entire remaining debt tenor:
$$\text{LLCR}_t = \frac{\sum_{j=t}^{T_{\text{loan}}} \frac{\text{CFADS}_j}{(1 + K_d)^{j-t+1}} + \text{Debt Service Reserve Fund}}{\text{Outstanding Debt}_t}$$
- **Institutional Standard:** Minimum $\text{LLCR} \ge 1.40x$.

### C. Project Life Coverage Ratio (PLCR):
Measures project cash generation over its entire economic lifecycle against initial debt:
$$\text{PLCR}_t = \frac{\sum_{j=t}^{T_{\text{project}}} \frac{\text{CFADS}_j}{(1 + K_d)^{j-t+1}}}{\text{Outstanding Debt}_t}$$

---

## 5. Industrial Break-Even Analysis
Determines minimum operational scale to prevent financial default:

$$\text{Break-Even Sales Revenue} = \frac{\text{Fixed Operating Costs}}{1 - \left(\frac{\text{Variable Costs}}{\text{Revenues}}\right)}$$

$$\text{Break-Even Capacity Utilization (\%)} = \frac{\text{Break-Even Sales}}{\text{Full Capacity Sales}} \times 100\%$$
- Industrial projects should target $< 60\%$ break-even capacity to withstand demand shocks.
