# 📘 McKinsey & Company: Valuation (8th Edition) Core Mathematical Reference

> **Primary Source:** *Valuation: Measuring and Managing the Value of Companies*, 8th Edition (Tim Koller, Marc Goedhart, David Wessels - McKinsey & Company, John Wiley & Sons).

---

## 1. The Fundamental Principle of Value Creation
A company creates value only when it earns a return on invested capital (**ROIC**) greater than its weighted average cost of capital (**WACC**), and grows. Growth creates more value when ROIC exceeds WACC, but destroys value when ROIC is below WACC.

---

## 2. The Key Value Driver Equation
The core valuation equation connects cash flow, growth, and returns:

$$\text{Value} = \frac{\text{NOPAT}_{t+1} \times \left(1 - \frac{g}{\text{ROIC}}\right)}{\text{WACC} - g}$$

Where:
- $\text{NOPAT}$: Net Operating Profit Less Adjusted Taxes.
- $g$: Perpetual growth rate of NOPAT and revenues.
- $\text{ROIC}$: Expected Return on New Invested Capital in perpetuity.
- $\frac{g}{\text{ROIC}}$: Investment Rate ($\text{IR}$), the portion of NOPAT that must be reinvested to sustain growth $g$.
- $\text{FCFF} = \text{NOPAT} \times (1 - \text{IR}) = \text{NOPAT} \times \left(1 - \frac{g}{\text{ROIC}}\right)$.

---

## 3. Net Operating Profit Less Adjusted Taxes (NOPAT)
NOPAT represents the total after-tax operating profit generated by the core business, regardless of capital structure (debt vs. equity):

$$\text{NOPAT} = \text{EBITA} - \text{Operating Cash Taxes}$$

### Mandatory Adjustments:
1. **Operating Lease Capitalization:** Add back implicit interest in operating lease expense to EBITA.
2. **R&D Capitalization:** R&D is an investment in long-term intangible assets, not an immediate operating expense. Capitalize R&D into Invested Capital and amortize over 3–7 years.
3. **Non-Operating Items:** Exclude interest income, dividend income from associates, FX gains/losses, non-operating provisions, and restructuring charges.

---

## 4. Invested Capital Reconciliation
Invested capital is the net operational assets required to generate NOPAT.

### Operating Approach (Assets):
$$\text{Invested Capital} = \text{Operating Cash} + \text{Operating Working Capital} + \text{Net PP\&E} + \text{Capitalized R\&D} + \text{Capitalized Leases} + \text{Other Operating Assets} - \text{Non-Interest-Bearing Current Liabilities (NIBCL)}$$

Where:
- **Operating Working Capital** = $(\text{Operating Receivables} + \text{Inventory} + \text{Prepaid Expenses}) - (\text{Operating Payables} + \text{Accrued Expenses})$.
- **Operating Cash**: Set at $1.5\% - 2.0\%$ of annual revenues (minimum required for daily store/factory operations); excess is treated as non-operating cash.

### Financing Approach (Liabilities & Equity):
$$\text{Invested Capital} = \text{Total Debt} + \text{Operating Lease Obligations} + \text{Retirement Deficits} + \text{Total Equity} - \text{Non-Operating Assets} - \text{Excess Cash}$$

Both approaches must reconcile with zero discrepancy:
$$\Delta = \text{Invested Capital}_{\text{Operating}} - \text{Invested Capital}_{\text{Financing}} \equiv 0$$

---

## 5. Return on Invested Capital (ROIC) Decomposition
ROIC measures operating efficiency independent of financing structure:

$$\text{ROIC} = \frac{\text{NOPAT}}{\text{Invested Capital}}$$

### Dupont-Style McKinsey Tree:
$$\text{ROIC} = \left(\frac{\text{NOPAT}}{\text{Revenues}}\right) \times \left(\frac{\text{Revenues}}{\text{Invested Capital}}\right)$$
$$\text{ROIC} = \text{Operating Margin} \times \text{Capital Turnover}$$

---

## 6. Economic Profit (EVA) Equivalence
Economic Profit measures value generated in excess of the cost of capital in a single period:

$$\text{Economic Profit}_t = \text{Invested Capital}_{t-1} \times (\text{ROIC}_t - \text{WACC})$$
$$\text{Economic Profit}_t = \text{NOPAT}_t - (\text{Invested Capital}_{t-1} \times \text{WACC})$$

### Valuation Equivalence Theorem:
Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) and Discounted Economic Profit yield identical Enterprise Values:
$$\text{Enterprise Value} = \text{Invested Capital}_0 + \sum_{t=1}^T \frac{\text{Economic Profit}_t}{(1 + \text{WACC})^t} + \frac{\text{Terminal Economic Profit}}{(1 + \text{WACC})^T}$$

---

## 7. Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)
$$\text{WACC} = \left(\frac{E}{V}\right) K_e + \left(\frac{D}{V}\right) K_d (1 - T)$$

Where:
- $E/V$ and $D/V$: Market value target weights (never book value weights).
- $K_e = R_f + \beta_L \times \text{ERP}$: Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM).
- $K_d$: Marginal pre-tax borrowing cost matching the currency and inflation of cash flows.
- $T$: Marginal corporate tax rate.

---

## 8. Enterprise Value to Equity Value Bridge
$$\text{Equity Value} = \text{Enterprise Value} + \text{Non-Operating Assets} - \text{Debt} - \text{Debt Equivalents} - \text{Non-Controlling Interests}$$

### Debt Equivalents:
- Capitalized operating lease liabilities (IFRS 16 / ASC 842).
- Net unfunded pension and post-retirement obligations.
- Expected value of pending litigation settlements.
- Decommissioning and environmental restoration provisions.
